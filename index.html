Australia's poultry industry

From growth to geography

This visual story explores how Australia's chicken meat and egg industries have changed over time, where production is concentrated, and how the industry's economic significance is developing.

01 — Long-term growth

Chicken meat production has expanded strongly over time

The story begins with the long-term trend. Production has generally increased, although some years show temporary slowdowns or declines.

What this shows

The visual highlights both overall production growth and year-to-year variation, helping separate the long-term direction from short-term fluctuations.

The long-term trend is clear — but what has happened more recently?
02 — Recent production

Recent production remains high

Recent quarterly data provide a closer look at chicken meat output, slaughter numbers and industry value.

Recent movement

This chart focuses on shorter-term changes, showing whether recent production continues the broader long-term growth story.

03 — Growth in volume and value

Industry growth is visible in both production and economic value

Physical output and monetary value capture two different dimensions of industry growth.

Production volume

This component compares the amount of chicken meat produced between the two reported periods.

Gross production value

This component shows how the value of chicken production changed over the same period.

04 — Geography

Chicken production is concentrated in major producing states

State-level data reveal where a large share of Australia's chicken meat production is concentrated.

State distribution

New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland are individually reported, while some other states are grouped in the source data.

Chicken meat is only one part of the poultry story. The next sections turn to eggs.
05 — Egg production

Egg production is concentrated across several major states

Comparing state shares shows how national egg production is distributed across Australia's major producing regions.

Overall distribution

The first part of the visual provides an overview of how national egg production is divided between the main producing states and the combined remainder.

Exact comparison

The bar chart makes the percentage differences easier to compare precisely across categories.

06 — Laying flock

The laying flock provides another perspective on egg production capacity

The distribution of laying hens helps show where the productive capacity of Australia's egg industry is located.

Different measure, different story

This visual represents each state's share of the national laying flock, so it should not be interpreted as direct egg output.

07 — Economic comparison

Chicken meat and eggs contribute at different economic scales

Comparing production value provides a simple economic perspective on the two major parts of Australia's poultry industry.

Economic scale

The comparison highlights the difference between poultry slaughtering value and egg production value.

08 — Future outlook

Forecasts point to continued expansion

Historical growth explains how the industry reached its current position. Forecasts provide a view of where production and exports may move next.

Looking forward

Forecast values extend the story beyond historical production and show the industry's expected future direction.

A poultry industry shaped by long-term growth

Together, these visualisations show sustained growth in chicken meat production, strong geographic concentration in both chicken and egg production, and continuing economic importance across Australia's poultry industry.
